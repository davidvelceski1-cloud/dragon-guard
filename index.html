How to Play

  • Dragons fly down 4 lanes toward the royal family on the castle wall.
  • 1. Tap a cannon to select it.
    2. Tap a lane or dragon to fire at the lowest dragon in that lane.
  • Match the dragon's colour:
    🔥 Red flame · 💧 Blue droplet
    🍃 Green leaf · ⭐ Yellow star
  • A wrong colour wastes the shot and makes the dragon angry and fast!
  • Armoured dragons change colour after each hit. Their rings show the colours still to come.
  • Ammo is limited. If you can't beat every dragon with what's left, the level is lost.
  • 3 correct hits in a row charge the 👑 Royal Volley: tap it to clear the lowest dragon in every lane.
  • Each dragon that reaches the wall costs a ❤️. Lose all 3 and the castle falls.
